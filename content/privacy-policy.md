## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Services, we may collect information about you as described below.

### A. Information You Provide

You provide us with information when you create an account, purchase story credits, create a storybook, or communicate with us. This may include:

- **Account Information.** Your email address and, if you sign in using a third-party authentication service such as Google, any information that service shares with us (typically your name and email address).
- **Child Character Information.** To create a personalized storybook, you provide a character name, gender selection, and a clear front-facing photograph of the child. This information is used solely to generate a personalized character and storybook illustrations.
- **Payment Information.** Payment details such as credit or debit card information and billing address, which are collected and processed by our third-party payment processors (currently Stripe for web purchases and RevenueCat for mobile purchases). We do not store full payment card numbers.
- **Communication Information.** Information contained in messages you send to us, such as support requests or feedback.

**YOU SHOULD NOT PROVIDE US WITH ANY CONFIDENTIAL, SENSITIVE, OR BIOMETRIC INFORMATION THROUGH THE SERVICE BEYOND WHAT IS REQUIRED TO CREATE A PERSONALIZED STORYBOOK.**

### B. Information Collected Automatically

We automatically collect certain information when you access or use the Services, including:

- **Device Data.** Device type, operating system, browser type and version, IP address, unique device identifiers, and mobile device ID.
- **Log and Usage Data.** Pages visited, features used, time and date of visits, time spent on pages, story credits purchased and used, storybooks created, and other diagnostic data.
- **Information Collected Through Tracking Technologies.** We may use cookies and similar technologies to collect some of the data discussed in this Privacy Policy. See the [Cookies and Similar Technologies](#5-cookies-and-similar-technologies) section below.

### C. Information from Third-Party Sources

We may receive information about you from third-party services you use to log into or otherwise connect to our Services, such as Google. The data we receive depends on that service's policies and your privacy settings with that service.

We may aggregate or de-identify information so that it can no longer identify you, as defined under applicable laws. We may use and disclose aggregated or de-identified information for any purpose unless applicable law says otherwise.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **Operate and Provide the Services.** To create personalized storybooks, manage your account, process transactions, and deliver the core functionality of the Services.
- **Process Payments.** To process purchases of story credits and any future products through our payment processors.
- **Generate Personalized Content.** To use the character name, gender, and photograph you provide to generate a personalized character and AI-generated storybook content (illustrations and text).
- **Monitor and Improve the Services.** To analyze the effectiveness of the Services, identify issues, and improve features and performance.
- **Communicate with You.** To send transactional communications such as purchase confirmations, account notifications, technical notices, security alerts, and support responses.
- **Facilitate a Corporate Transaction.** To use information during negotiations or in connection with a merger, acquisition, sale of assets, or similar transaction.
- **Foster Safety and Security.** To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and other harmful activities, and to protect the security of the Services and our users.
- **Comply with Legal Obligations.** To comply with applicable laws, legal processes, and lawful requests from authorities.

**Important — No AI Training on Your Data.** We do not use child photographs, character information, or any user-submitted content to train or improve artificial intelligence or machine learning models. User-submitted content is used solely to generate the specific storybook you request.

## 10. Your EEA Privacy Rights

If you reside in the EEA, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"):

- **Access.** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction.** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
- **Erasure.** Request deletion of your personal data where there is no legitimate reason for us to continue processing it.
- **Objection.** Object to processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.
- **Restriction.** Request that we suspend processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.
- **Portability.** Request transfer of your personal data to you or a third party in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
- **Withdraw Consent.** Withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent to process your data, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@twinklebot.com. We will respond to legitimate requests within one month, or up to three months for complex requests.

## 14. Children's Privacy

TwinkleBot is a service designed to create personalized storybooks for children; however, the Services are intended to be used by parents and legal guardians, not by children directly. **You must be at least 18 years old to create an account and use the Services.**

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). The child character information (name, gender, and photograph) is provided by the parent or legal guardian, who represents and warrants that they have the legal authority to provide this information.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us at support@twinklebot.com and we will promptly take steps to delete such information.
